ODS DOCX

Spreadsheet to Word

Open an .ods spreadsheet and get a Word document with each sheet as a table. Your file stays on this device.

Install it on your iPhone

Tap the Share button in Safari, then Add to Home Screen. It will then open like an app and work offline.
